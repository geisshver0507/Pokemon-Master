How to Play the Game
1. Choose Pokemon
	- You will be prompted to select two distinct Pokemon by entering their numbers (0 to 4)
	- CHoose a Pokemon (0-4)
	- If you choose the same Pokemon twice, the program will terminate
2. Battle
	- Each Pokemon takes turns to attack.
	- You will be prompted to choose a skill by entering its number (0 to 3)
	- Choose a skill (0-3)
	- The effectiveness of the skill will be displayed, and the opponent's HP will be updated.
3. Victory
	- The game ends when one Pokemon's HP reaches 0 or below.
	- The result of the battle will be displayed.
